Sounds were converted from the original OGG files to 22.05 kHz mono 16-bit
WAV (ffmpeg) and are RMS-normalised at load. Sprites are used as shipped
(downscaled 2x at load). Road surfaces, kerbs, barriers, lighting, particles,
UI panels, engine sounds and music are generated by the game's own code.
