# Night Signal — self-review

Compared with Lanternfall, the current best-rated game (7/10 overall; art style explicitly praised).

| Axis | Lanternfall player feedback | Night Signal self-score | Assessment |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Fun | 7/10 | 4/10 | Broadcast choices and relay matching are understandable, but the campaign resolves into repetitive menu selections instead of richer management decisions. |
| Visuals | loved the art style | 4/10 | A coherent pixel palette and city silhouette are present, but the map is sparse, the districts are text labels, and there is no character animation, although the score and broadcast stingers now play in the windowed game. |
| Replayability | would replay and share | 3/10 | Seeded district needs and multiple programs offer some variation; station upgrades carry through the campaign, but there are no authored event branches or meaningfully different endings. |
| Controls | movement felt tricky | 5/10 | Mouse and keyboard inputs are simple, but some controls lack feedback and upgrades are not explained in the live interface. |

This does **not** beat Lanternfall yet. The primary weaknesses are shallow progression and limited audio reactivity, both below the written daily bar. The simulated selftest confirms that a trust-preserving ending and a rumor-driven loss are reachable, but does not establish that the game is fun for a full sitting. The screenshots were inspected at 1280×720; the HUD and ending are now kept within the frame, although the map needs more visual variety.
